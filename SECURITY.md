# Security policy

## Reporting a hole

Please report it privately, never in a public issue.

- **In this template:** GitHub's private reporting, under the repository's
  Security tab ("Report a vulnerability").
- **In a site built from it:** the `Contact:` in that site's
  `/.well-known/security.txt`.
- **In Loco, Leptos or another dependency:** to that project, as its own
  policy asks.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it lets someone do. You'll
get an answer, and credit if you want it once the fix is out.

## Supported

The `main` branch. Fixes land there; there are no release branches.

## What the template checks

`docs/security.md`: what it defends against, what was reviewed and its
status, the risks it accepts, and what an app must add as it grows.
